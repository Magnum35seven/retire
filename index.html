Rule update policy

  1. Detect and record source changes.
  2. Check the change against the applicable official instrument or publication.
  3. Record the effective date and the exact changed value or logic.
  4. Run regression tests and edge-case tests.
  5. Publish a new ruleset version with a change log; retain prior versions.

Automated monitoring can flag changes, but should not silently infer the legal effect of a changed webpage.
