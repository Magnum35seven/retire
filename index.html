Monthly cash-flow model: investment returns compound monthly, withdrawals are timed at the start or end of each month, and contributions are spread across the year until retirement. Tax estimates use 2026-27 resident brackets with approximate LITO, SAPTO and Medicare levy treatment; couple taxable income is split equally because individual allocations are not captured. Age Pension is estimated annually from approximate mid-year balances. Fees, Medicare levy calculations, offsets, minimum drawdowns, account-based pension exceptions, Work Bonus, Rent Assistance and many Centrelink special rules are not fully modelled.

Age Pension estimate

Standard Australian resident rules, effective 20 September 2026 where stated. Estimate excludes Work Bonus, transitional rates, Rent Assistance, overseas payment rules, compensation, gifting, income-stream exceptions, asset exemptions and other special circumstances.

Enter assessable figures and calculate an estimate.
Couples where only one partner is 67+: the eligible person is still assessed as a member of a couple, not as a single person. Use the couple income test and combined asset thresholds. Count both partners’ assessable assets, but ordinarily exclude the under-age partner’s super held in accumulation phase if it is not paying an income stream. Include it if Centrelink rules make it assessable; income streams may be treated differently. The under-age partner’s wages and other assessable income can still matter. This tool cannot determine every super/income-stream exception, so enter the combined assessable totals after checking the official guidance.

Rule update policy

  1. Detect and record source changes.
  2. Check the change against the applicable official instrument or publication.
  3. Record the effective date and the exact changed value or logic.
  4. Run regression tests and edge-case tests.
  5. Publish a new ruleset version with a change log; retain prior versions.

Automated monitoring can flag changes, but should not silently infer the legal effect of a changed webpage.
